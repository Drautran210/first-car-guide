Quick Buying Checklist for Bulgaria (Mobile.bg / Cars.bg)

Four practical checks before finalizing any used compact car in Bulgaria:

1. Underbody & Arch Rust ("Nov vnos")

Many imports come from Austria, Germany, or Switzerland where heavy winter road salt is used. Inspect rear wheel arches and sills carefully.

2. Gearbox Selection

If buying an automatic, prioritize Mazda 2, Nissan Micra K12, or Hyundai i20. Avoid early single-clutch semi-autos (Toyota MMT / Honda i-SHIFT).

3. Annual Tax & GO Insurance

Petrol engines under 1.4L (70–95 hp) fall into the lowest municipal road tax and Civil Liability (GO) insurance brackets in Bulgaria.

4. Valid GTP & Service Basics

For cars already registered in Bulgaria, verify valid GTP (annual technical inspection) and check tire date codes for winter readiness.
