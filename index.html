Smart Buyer's Checklist Around Yambol & Bulgaria

Key checks when viewing cars in Yambol, Sliven (~28 km), Stara Zagora (~82 km), or Burgas (~92 km):

1. Underbody & Arch Rust ("Нов внос")

Many imports come from Austria, Germany, or Switzerland where winter road salt is heavy. Check rear wheel arches, sills, and exhaust lines carefully.

2. Gearbox Selection

For an automatic, prioritize Mazda 2, Nissan Micra K12, Hyundai i10/i20, Opel Corsa D (1.4 Auto), or Honda Jazz (CVT/Auto). Avoid single-clutch semi-autos (MMT, i-SHIFT, Easytronic, Dualogic, Allshift).

3. Annual Tax, GO & Vignette

Petrol engines under 1.4L (65–95 hp) get the lowest municipal road tax and GO insurance. Annual BG highway vignette is ~€45–€50/year.

4. Valid GTP & First Service Budget

Verify valid ГТП (GTP) on registered cars, and always keep ~€250–€350 aside for fresh oil, filters, and a quick mechanic check after buying.
